Common questions

Before you apply

Does UniCou guarantee admission or a visa?

No. Admission decisions are made by universities and immigration decisions by the relevant authorities. No result can be guaranteed.

Are the university programs and fees shown here current?

This starter website does not publish program fees or promise availability. Confirm current program details, fees, deadlines and entry requirements on official university websites.

Can I apply before choosing a university?

Yes, you can submit an enquiry first and use it to discuss your interests and questions before deciding which options to research further.

Does the online form save my application?

Not in this demo. It prepares a WhatsApp message on your device. A secure production application system needs a backend, access controls, privacy notice and secure data storage.
