Frequently asked questions

Before making a decision

Can UniCou guarantee admission or a visa?

No. Universities decide admissions and the relevant authorities decide immigration outcomes. Neither can be guaranteed.

Are fees and program details guaranteed to be current?

No fixed prices are listed here. Confirm fees, program availability, deadlines and entry requirements directly with the institution.

What documents will I need?

Requirements vary by institution, course and personal circumstances. Request a current checklist from the university and check official immigration instructions.

Does this form securely store my application?

No. This demo saves a local copy in the current browser and prepares a WhatsApp message. A production system needs secure server-side storage, access controls and a privacy policy.
